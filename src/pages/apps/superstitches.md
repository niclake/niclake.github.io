As a baseball fan, I am extremely superstitious about the hats and jerseys I wear on game day. Team's on a hot streak? Keep running it back. Just dropped 3 in a row? That hat's going to the back of the drawer.

But what about the win/loss record for a jersey for the entire season? What combo of hat & jersey works out best? And how on earth are you supposed to keep track of it all?

Enter **SuperStitches** - an app born of my own desire to have all of those metrics readily available to me at a moment's notice, without needing to punch information into a spreadsheet.

You can [download SuperStitches from the App Store][sslink].

## Current Features

- Standings for your MLB hats, jerseys, and each unique combination you wear, along with an easy way to share your standings with others
- A wardrobe section to keep track of all of your items, along with the ability to retire them as they wear out
- A full schedule for your team, showing all of the games & what was worn for each
- Themes based on team colorways - a team's home colorway is available by default, with all others available from a one-time in-app purchase
- Notifications to remind you to track your fit, & update you with the results
- A Recommended Fit suggestion, helping you pair your hats & jerseys each day
- Separate W/L tracking for games you see in person

## Planned Features

- More leagues (NBA, NFL, NHL, MLS, WPBL, and more)
- Multiple favorite teams, both in the same league + across multiple leagues
- Additional item types like shoes or hoodies
- Apple Watch integration

### Why did you make this?

In 2026, I decided to keep track of the W/L records of all of my hats & jerseys, but I genuinely got so annoyed at having to punch all of this information into a spreadsheet each day that I made this app.

### How much does SuperStitches cost?

All functional features of SuperStitches are, and always will be, free to use.

I have a bunch of future enhancements that I want to make to expand functionality and customization, and when that happens, I'll likely put those features behind a $5, one-time-purchase paywall. I generally don't care for subscription models, and I want you to be able to own the app.

A tip jar will also be implemented so that you're able to support me beyond that purchase, if you see fit. Until then, you can [buy me a coffee](https://ko-fi.com/niclake){:target="_blank"}
