**I switched e-readers**

I've been using a Kindle Paperwhite for a while now, and it was fine. But I'm trying as best I can to distance myself from Amazon, and their changes to e-books over the years finally pushed me in that direction.

Previously, you were able to download books that you had purchased as epubs, or some Amazon-specific e-book format. Then they added DRM, which meant you had to figure out workarounds to crack books you legally owned so you could read them elsewhere (or back them up). Then in the fall of 2025, they removed that ability for you to download your e-books entirely.

So I said fuck it, and picked up a [Kobo Clara Colour](https://us.kobobooks.com/products/kobo-clara-colour?variant=43784716714054).

So far, it's been magnificent. The Kobos let you tinker a bit more, and you can add plugins to them relatively easily. I have 2; one that shows the clock, battery, and both the current page + % progress in the book, as well as one that links directly with [Hardcover](https://hardcover.app/@cinekal) (my reading tracker of choice) to update my reading progress. This *should* mean that [my bookshelf](https://niclake.me/catalog/bookshelf/) should now be fully automated (and I could even add my current book progress on there as well).

I'm currently behind a bit on my goal of reading 25 books this year (I've read 14 so far, and "pace" should be about 16-17), BUT I have the spark again, and a big list of books to get to.

**App development is easy; app marketing is hard**

Coming up with this app idea, and executing on the plan, was actually a really fun challenge with a clear goal in sight. I've also gotten some great feedback from a few of my early testers, which has helped me fix a lot of bugs I wasn't aware of.

But now comes the hard part: telling everyone else about the app.

I still have a few more hurdles that I have to get over. My developer account can't accept payments until I clear something up with their Finance department, or make an LLC. As such, my app can't be submitted to the App Store proper. I still need a not shitty icon, but good icons cost A LOT, and I have no idea how much this app will actually bring in (if anything), and it's terrifying to spend that much on what would then just be a little passion project.

Anyway... I can see the finish line. Now I just gotta keep the wheels turning and get there.
