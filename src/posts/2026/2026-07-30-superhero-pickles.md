Everyone please meet Superhero Pickles.

---

We've been mulling over getting a 3rd cat for a while now. [Pixel passed away right after we moved](/pixel/), and for the last 3 years, we've been a 2 cat household. Bearcat (now 15) didn't mind this, but Beau (now 5) was clearly bored. He'd always try to get someone to play with him, bother Bearcat, and just generally seem lonely.

In early June, as I was making a grocery run, I decided to stop in our local PetSmart. Always nice to see some kittens. One thing led to another, and my wife & daughter ran out to meet one of the cats, a little guy named Scotty. We ended up filling out an adoption application, and had it sent in.

That email got swallowed up by spam filters. Scotty got adopted by someone else.

But! The woman running the shelter was able to send us a bunch of pictures of other cats. She had about 2 dozen kittens at the time, and was happy to let us meet them all. So on Saturday, we took a drive. The shelter is about an hour north of us, and we barely made it there ahead of a massive thunderstorm.

Now to preface a bit - up until this point, my daughter was ambivelent about cats. When she'd gone to PetSmart to meet Scotty, she seemed more interested in the cat toys than anything else.

We walked into this shelter, and watched her face completely light up. "Mommy, all the kitties are meowing at me! Daddy look, this one has different colored eyes! Can we get them all and they can sleep in my bed?" She also did a perfect reenactment of "human attempts to corral multiple kittens who do not want to be corralled" and it was hilarious.

We met this orange cat Gregory, and he was immediately so loving, snuggling with all 3 of us. We knew he was perfect for our home. 

On the ride home, we tried to brainstorm names with our daughter, tossing out some good ones we'd thought of already. Miso, Cheeto, Biscuit, Kwazii, and more. When we asked her, she practically shouted out "SUPERHERO PICKLES!!!", and she would not be budged from her stance. So, I promised her that his government name could be Superhero Pickles (but that we'd just call him Pickles).

We intended on picking him up on July 9th, but he'd gotten his vet clearance early, so I snuck up on the 7th and brought him home, to the surprise and delight of everyone.

---

Things haven't been flawless. He and Beau warmed to each other quickly, but Pickles is constantly in "play" mode when everyone else (humans AND cats) are in "rest" mode. Bearcat is anti-kitten to the max, but Pickles doesn't back down from the whappy-paws, and keeps trying. Last night, when I was watching baseball, he spent most of the game biting me because I guess that's his idea of a good time.

But on night 1, Pickles crawled under the covers next to my daughter and fell asleep all night. Yesterday, he spent most of the day with her in a laundry basket hanging out. Beau woke up from his nap, and was looking all over the house trying to find him, so clearly there's a bond already established there.
