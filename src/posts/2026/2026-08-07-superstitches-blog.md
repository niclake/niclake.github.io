Any die-hard baseball fan that you know is at least a little superstitious.

This is especially true if they used to play baseball to some degree. The way they step up to the plate. The way they run on and off the field. The way they tie their cleats.

Being a fan is no different. 162 games across 6+ months is an absolute grind, and a bit of consistency helps make that a more normal part of your life. You gotta have the TV (or radio) on. You sit in the same spot on the couch. If you go to the games, you get the same food from the same vendors at the same time during the game.

My superstitious tic is that I will wear a hat or a jersey until there are no more wins left in them.

This season, I made a spreadsheet[^1] to track the win/loss record of all of my hats and jerseys. Then I color coded it. Then I started tracking all the unique hat/jersey combos. It was fun to have all that data, but it had its downsides:

- Sometimes I'd forget to update it for a few days, and had to remember what I wore
- Entering data on the computer was easy, but punching it in on my phone was obnoxious
- I was manually tracking the Sox' schedule & results; I got some of the dates wrong, handling the couple of rain delays was weird... it just wasn't elegant.

[^1]: Because of course I did... I love spreadsheets just as much as I love Red Sox baseball.

I remember thinking one day, "this would be so much nicer if it was just on my phone". I've had ideas for some other iPhone apps in the past, but nothing that I actually chose to pursue beyond jotting down some notes. This one, though... this one had the sauce.

And now here we are in early August. While I'm still a little ways off from an App Store release, [my first app, SuperStitches, is in beta / TestFlight stage](/superstitches/).

I'm actually super happy with how this app came together, and I'm really excited about future developments I have planned. It's also been fun to put my mind to a side project like this, and actually see it through.

I don't anticipate getting rich off of this by any means, but it's still something I can hang my hat on & toss on my resume, which will be nice.

If this sounds like something you're interested in, [check out the SuperStitches app page](/superstitches/) for details on how to snag it.
